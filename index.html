How it works

  • Paste rows from each supplier spreadsheet, or type them in the same column order.
  • Per-unit normalisation divides pack price by pack size so a 500-sheet pack and a 100-sheet pack compare fairly.
  • MOQ and lead-time columns sit beside every price, with the best comparable per-unit price highlighted per row.
  • Landed totals for your target quantity include delivery and whole-pack rounding.
  • Rows quoted in different units are flagged non-comparable instead of silently mis-ranked, and the grid exports as CSV.
